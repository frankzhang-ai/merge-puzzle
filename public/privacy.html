MERGE BLOOM – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026.

No account

Merge Bloom does not require an account. Your progress, album, and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a cookie-less analytics service, to count page views and anonymous game events such as level starts, clears, failures, daily plays, and return visits. We do not use cross-site tracking or advertising IDs. Read GoatCounter’s privacy information.

Advertising

Merge Bloom currently shows no ads. A disabled rewarded-ad hook may be present in the game, but it does not load or show an advertisement.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask players for personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the game changes. The effective date above will be revised when we do. Questions can be sent to playonesecond@proton.me.
